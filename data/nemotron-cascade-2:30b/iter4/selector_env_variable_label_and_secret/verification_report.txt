Findings summary
- The Pod selector-env-secret-app is Running and Ready, but the Service selector-env-secret-service has no Endpoints due to a selector mismatch.
- There is a persistent Secret named selector-env-secret; the container can read the APP_MESSAGE value inside the Pod, and the app responds at 127.0.0.1:8080, but the Service itself is not routing to the Pod because its selector does not match the Pod labels.
- Because the Service is ClusterIP and its Endpoints are empty, there is no external reachability via the Service. Internal reachability to the Pod is possible, but the Service path is broken.

Root cause analysis based on observations
- The primary service misconfiguration is the selector mismatch:
  - Pod labels: app=selector-env-secret-app
  - Service selector: app=wrong-selector
  - Consequently, the Service has no Endpoints, so the Service cannot route to the running Pod.
- The Secret key error (cannot find key message) appears in Pod events, but the application does successfully read APP_MESSAGE at runtime (as evidenced by the internal 127.0.0.1:8080 response). The internal health suggests the secret is available to the Pod, but the service path remains broken due to label mismatch.

Conclusion
- The issue described in the original problem is not fully resolved in the current cluster state. While the Pod is Running and the application can read the secret internally, the Service has no working endpoints due to a selector mismatch, and external access via the Service is not possible in its current form. Therefore, this does not satisfy “the Service has no working endpoints after the container issue is addressed” as resolved.
